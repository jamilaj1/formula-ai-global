What you get

A report you can hand to your factory

Typical report structure (Full Formulation Report)

  1. Executive summary — verdict, feasibility, expected cost at a glance
  2. Ingredient list — name, percentage, CAS, function, $/kg, suggested supplier
  3. Safety analysis — GHS classification, known incompatibilities, required PPE, storage
  4. Cost analysis — batch cost at 1 kg / 100 kg / 1 t, biggest line items, money-saving substitutions
  5. Stability prediction — estimated shelf life, pH/temperature/light sensitivity, recommended tests
  6. Regulatory status — EU + US-FDA + GCC status, required warning labels, any export restrictions
  7. Preparation steps — order of addition, mixing speeds, temperatures, checkpoints
  8. Quality control — per-batch tests + acceptable ranges
  9. Chemist signature — name, date, review-log reference number

💡 Real sample report: We send you an example matched to your industry (industrial cleaning, cosmetics, food, etc.) as soon as we receive your brief. We don't publish generic samples because every project is different.
