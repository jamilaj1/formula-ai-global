## Posting tips
- Upload video NATIVELY to Facebook (don't paste a YouTube link).
- First line is everything — it's the hook before "see more".
- Reply to every comment in the first hour (FB rewards engagement → reach).
- Pin the best-performing post to the top of your page.
- Best times for B2B/industrial audiences: weekday mornings (their time).
- Never fabricate numbers/testimonials (Post 11) — real proof only.
